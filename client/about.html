About this monitor

A focused weather instrument for one place

The Malolos Weather Monitor tracks live conditions for Malolos, Bulacan, and presents them the way a local would want to read them — in Philippine time, with the monsoon in mind.

Where the numbers come from

Every value on this site comes from a live weather API. Nothing is invented or hard-coded to look plausible — if a reading can't be fetched, the section says so plainly instead of showing a fake number.

WeatherAPI.com

Current conditions, the hourly forecast, air quality, sunrise and sunset, and any official alerts. These need an API key configured on the server; without one, those sections show a clear "unavailable" notice.

Open-Meteo

The 7-day forecast and recent history. Open-Meteo is keyless, so the forecast, history, and derived advisories keep working even when the current-conditions provider isn't configured.

How advisories are made

Alerts on this site come in three layers, kept visually and textually distinct so you always know what you're looking at:

  1. Official alerts — passed through unchanged from the weather provider when a warning is in effect for the area.
  2. Derived advisories — plain-language notes our system generates from forecast thresholds (for example, sustained heavy rain or a high heat index). These are labeled "not official" everywhere they appear.
  3. Official channels — a permanent link to PAGASA, shown whether or not any alert is active.
Derived advisories are a convenience, not a warning service. For decisions about safety, follow PAGASA and your local disaster risk reduction office.

Times & units

All timestamps are shown in Philippine Standard Time (UTC+8), no matter your device's time zone. Temperatures default to Celsius; switch to Fahrenheit any time from the header — the conversion happens in your browser.

Accounts & privacy

Accounts are optional. Signed out, your unit and theme choices are saved locally in your browser. Signed in, those same preferences sync to your account — that's all an account stores. If the server has no database configured, accounts are hidden and the app works exactly as before.
